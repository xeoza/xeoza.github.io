03 — Roles

The attorney runs the case, we remove the admin

The boundary of responsibility is clear before the agreement is signed.

Attorney

  • Gives a legal assessment of the situation
  • Prepares procedural documents
  • Represents you in court and before authorities
  • Is responsible for the legal position

Coordinator

  • Takes the enquiry and clarifies details
  • Reviews the policy and documents
  • Translates and manages the document flow
  • Keeps deadlines and reports

The legal services agreement is with the attorney directly. Before signing, you meet them and can verify their bar registration.

09 — Вопросы

Коротко о главном

Покрывает ли российский полис Турцию?

Проверяем по каждому полису: территория, лимиты, франшиза. Если покрытия нет, скажем до начала работы.

Сколько это стоит?

Зависит от объёма. Сумму называем до старта, после проверки документов и полиса.

На каком языке общаемся?

По-русски, по-английски или по-турецки. Документы для органов — на турецком.

С кем я подписываю договор?

С адвокатом — на юридические услуги. Условия координации фиксируем отдельно.

09 — Questions

The essentials

Does a Russian policy cover Türkiye?

We check each policy: territory, limits, deductible. If there is no coverage, we say so before work starts.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the scope. We give the amount before work starts, after reviewing the documents and policy.

What language do we use?

Russian, English or Turkish. Documents for authorities are in Turkish.

Who do I sign the agreement with?

The attorney — for legal services. Coordination terms are fixed separately.

09 — Sorular

Kısaca önemli olanlar

Rusya poliçesi Türkiye'yi kapsar mı?

Her poliçeyi kontrol ederiz: bölge, limitler, muafiyet. Teminat yoksa işe başlamadan söyleriz.

Ne kadar tutar?

Kapsama bağlı. Tutarı işe başlamadan, belgeler ve poliçe incelendikten sonra söyleriz.

Hangi dilde görüşüyoruz?

Rusça, İngilizce veya Türkçe. Kurumlar için belgeler Türkçe.

Sözleşmeyi kiminle imzalıyorum?

Hukuki hizmet için avukatla. Koordinasyon koşulları ayrıca sabitlenir.

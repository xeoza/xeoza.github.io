Four gaps between the client and the outcome

A Russian-speaking client in Türkiye faces four issues: language, procedure, insurance, organisation.

02 — Model

What the coordination company does

We are not a law firm. We organise the process: intake, documents, attorney, deadlines.

What we do

  • Intake and qualification
  • Policy and document checks
  • Partner attorney selection
  • Translation and document flow
  • Deadlines and communication
  • Case reporting

What we do not do

  • Provide legal services
  • Replace the attorney
  • Guarantee an outcome
  • Decide for the client

We do not replace the attorney

Legal work is done by a bar-registered attorney.

Transparent fee split

The two fees are separate and visible before work starts.

Confidentiality

Attorney-client privilege and KVKK; data shared only with written consent.

One point of accountability

One coordinator owns process and deadlines.

03 — Scenarios

Three flows, three payment schemes

The flows differ in who pays and who handles the case.

Client with legal expenses insurance

Bought in Russia. Applicability is checked per policy: territory, limits, deductible, approval.

Who pays
The insurer covers the company fee; the attorney fee follows the policy
Who handles the case
A partner attorney in Türkiye

Client without insurance

Direct to the company, or through a partner law firm in Moscow.

Who pays
The client — coordination fee and attorney fee
Who handles the case
A Turkish partner attorney

Turkish client with a case in Russia

Reverse flow: the case runs in Russia.

Who pays
The client
Who handles the case
A partner law firm in Russia

04 — Process

Six steps from enquiry to closure

Identical for every flow.

  1. Enquiry

    An email or call with a short description.

  2. Qualification

    We check fit and conflicts of interest.

  3. Policy and documents

    Coverage, limits, deductible and approval.

  4. Attorney selection

    A partner attorney registered with the bar.

  5. Handling and reporting

    The attorney runs the case; we coordinate.

  6. Closure

    Outcome, documents and feedback.

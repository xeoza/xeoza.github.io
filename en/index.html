02 — How it works

The attorney runs the case, we remove the admin

We are not a law firm and not an intermediary without authority. Our job is to keep the case moving: documents translated, deadlines under control, insurer and attorney speaking to you through one person.

What the attorney does

  • Gives a legal assessment of the situation
  • Prepares procedural documents
  • Represents you in court and before authorities
  • Is responsible for the legal position

What we do

  • Take the enquiry and clarify the details
  • Review the policy and documents
  • Translate and manage the document flow
  • Keep deadlines and report

You sign the legal services agreement directly with the attorney. Before signing, you meet them and can verify their bar registration.

05 — Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does my Russian policy cover expenses in Türkiye?

We check each policy: territory, limits, deductible and the insurer's approval procedure. If there is no coverage, we say so before work starts.

Do documents need translation?

Yes. Proceedings in Türkiye are in Turkish. Translation and certification are our part; the attorney works with ready documents.

How much does it cost?

The coordination fee and the attorney fee are separate. We give the exact amount before work starts, after reviewing the documents and the policy; it depends on the scope and complexity.

Who do I sign the agreement with?

The legal services agreement is with the attorney directly. Coordination terms are fixed separately, before work starts.

What language do we use?

Russian, English or Turkish. Documents and correspondence with Turkish authorities are in Turkish and translated for you.
